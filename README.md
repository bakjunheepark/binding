# Binding

Binding reads three essays on the typographic grid side by side: Anthony Froshaug's *Typography is a Grid* (1967), Richard Hollis's *The Modernist Grid* (2004), and Rob Giampietro's *Unbuilding* (2012). Together they trace the grid from its origins in metal type, through its systematisation in Modernist design, to its loosening on the responsive web. Each essay is set on its own variation of one shared grid system, built from HTML and CSS only and set entirely in Satoshi.

The pages share a common structure of thin-ruled boxes, red pull quotes and a title-block footer, while each title and its details respond to its text: stepped black blocks for Froshaug, rectangle studies for Hollis, and dashed blank squares marking what Giampietro's talk removes or leaves unbuilt. The landing page arranges the three essays as a checkerboard, with an Intro and Colophon that open without JavaScript.

Designed and coded by Jun Park for *Typography & Interaction*, 2026–2027.
