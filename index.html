Binding, three essays on the grid

The Grid,Built & Unbuilt

Intro Every typographic page rests on a grid, whether we see it or not. These three essays look at how that structure was built, systematised, and finally loosened.
Colophon Binding was designed and coded by Jun Park as a study of the typographic grid, built from HTML and CSS. It reads three essays side by side: Anthony Froshaug’s Typography is a Grid (1967), Richard Hollis’s The Modernist Grid (2004), and Rob Giampietro’s Unbuilding (2012). Each is set on its own variation of the grid, in Satoshi and Erode.
